How to Install as an App on Mobile & Laptop

Follow the quick steps below for your device to get an app icon on your home screen!

Android (Chrome / Brave)
  1. Tap the 3 dots (⋮) menu in the top right corner of Chrome.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Confirm by clicking Install.
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this page in Safari browser.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
Laptop / PC (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Click the Install Icon in the browser URL address bar.
  2. Or click the "Install App" button at the top header!
